▤ Receipt‑First

The words on the screen are hashed into the transaction you sign. Devnet only.

Every wallet asks you to sign a hex blob.
This one hands you a receipt, then staples it to the chain.

  1. 1 Pick a taste. The taste is what gets minted.
  2. 2 Read the receipt. Every lamport, in plain English.
  3. 3 Sign. The receipt's SHA‑256 rides along in the memo and in the token's own on-chain metadata.
  4. 4 Anyone re-derives the receipt from the chain and checks the hash. Including you, in the Verify tab.

The verifier does not trust this app's memory. It fetches the confirmed transaction, decodes the instructions with the same decoder used before signing, regenerates every sentence, hashes the result, and compares it with the hash the transaction carries. If a single lamport of disclosed cost differs, the hashes diverge.

  1. 1 Fetch the confirmed transaction from the cluster.
  2. 2 Decode all of its instructions with the same decoder used before signing.
  3. 3 Regenerate the receipt and hash it.
  4. 4 Compare that hash with the one the transaction carries — and compare the quote with what the chain actually took.
